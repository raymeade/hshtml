FIRST ANNUAL GOLF OUTING

HYSA is excited to announce that we will be having our FIRST ANNUAL GOLF OUTING THIS YEAR!

The outing is set to take place on August 17th, 2023, at Pinelands Golf Club in Hammonton, New Jersey 08037. It's open to the public- players may register as teams or individuals. Cost is $125/player, $500 per foursome. The following is included in the fees: full day of golf, carts for all, score sheets, lunch and more!

HYSA is a 501c3 non-profit organization, working to raise funds that will allow us the opportunity to provide updated equipment for our players, fans, and entire community. The money will go towards: new goals and light towers, players benches at the fields, new equipment and access to the most current safety and first aid procedures and much more!

Here is the information (registration and sponsorship), if you have any questions feel free to reach out to HYSA president Antonio Illiano at hysagolfouting@gmail.com
