About Mini Novice

Director: Antonio

Email Address

The Mini Novice program is to further the development of players as individuals while also expanding their ability to play as a team. Skills such as dribbling, passing, and shooting should be taught as individual technical skills as well as passing as a simple team skill. Additional structures out of bounds are also introduced from the real game. Parents should cheer for BOTH teams and ALL players in a supportive environment versus a competitive one. For all games, the scheduled game time is the time the game should start. Please arrive at the fields early to warm up.

MINI NOVICE RULES:

The Ball: Size 4

General Rules

  • Number of Players: 7v7 or 8v8 depending on roster size
  • Players for both teams will be on the one side of the field. Parents sit on the outside around the fields
  • Out ­of ­bounds on sidelines results in a throw in for the opposing team.
  • Out­ of ­bounds on goal lines result in goal kick (ball off offensive team) or corner kick (ball off defensive team)
  • All fouls / infringements are Indirect. Defending team must be at least 5 yards (15 feet) from the ball when it is kicked
  • NO SLIDE TACKLING. This is dangerous for players at this age.

Corner Kicks

  • A corner kick is awarded when the ball crosses the (goal line) and was last touched by the defending team
  • The ball may be placed anywhere within or on the arc defining the corner area
  • Defending team must be at least 5 yards (15 feet) from the ball when it is kicked

Goal Kicks

  • A goal kick is awarded when the whole of the ball crosses the goal line and was last touched by the offensive team
  • Goal kicks may be taken anywhere on the goal line, typically within a few feet of the goal posts.
  • Defensive team must be at least 10 yards from goal kick

Substitutions

  • Prior permission of the referee is required in order to make any substitution at any time.

Payers Equipment -
Players required to wear their issued jersey, Shin guards (must be covered by sock) and issued socks. Cleats are optional but are highly recommended as are black soccer shorts. No Jewelry of any kind is allowed. Players with glasses must either remove them or have approved sports goggles to play.

The Referee – Referees and players on field (no coaches)

Match Duration – Teams will play four 8 minute quarters, 5 minute half plus 2 minutes between quarters.

Notes for coaches
  • Rocommended: Practices once per week for 45-60 minutes
  • Good sportsmanship, fun and learning are a priority
  • Code of Conduct must be followed
As a reminder, HYSA is a recreational league. If your team is much stronger than the other team, please find creative ways to make the match fairer and ensure that both teams are having fun.
